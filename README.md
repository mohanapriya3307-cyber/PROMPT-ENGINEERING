1. Introduction
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, decision-making and understanding language.

Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI) is an advanced branch of AI that can create new content such as text, images, videos, music and computer code.

Today, Generative AI is widely used in education, healthcare, business, entertainment and software development.

Examples:
- ChatGPT – Generates human-like text and answers.
- Google Gemini – Generates text and helps with problem-solving.
- DALL·E – Creates images from text descriptions.
- GitHub Copilot – Generates programming code.
2. Definition of Generative AI
Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that learns patterns and information from existing data and uses that knowledge to generate new and original content.

Simple definition: Generative AI is a technology that creates new content based on the instructions or prompts given by users.

For example:
- Input: "Write a poem about nature."
- Output: A newly generated poem about nature.

3. Working Principle of Generative AI
Generative AI works by learning patterns from large amounts of training data and using those patterns to generate new content.

The working principle involves the following steps:
1. Data Collection: The AI system collects large amounts of data, such as text, images, audio and videos.
2. Training: The model learns patterns, relationships and structures from the collected data.
3. User Input: The user provides an instruction called a prompt.
4. Processing: The trained model understands and processes the given prompt.
5. Content Generation: The model generates new content based on its learning.
6. Output: The generated content is displayed to the user.

5. Key Components of Generative AI
The major components of Generative AI are:

1. Training Data
- Large collections of text, images, audio and other information.
- Used to teach the AI model.
2. Machine Learning
- Enables computers to learn patterns from data.
- Helps the model improve its ability to generate content.
3. Neural Networks
- Computing systems inspired by the human brain.
- Help identify complex patterns and relationships in data.
4. Generative Models
- Algorithms that learn data patterns and generate new content.
- Examples include GANs, VAEs and Transformers.
5. User Prompt
- An instruction or question given to the AI system.
- Guides the type of content to be generated.

1. Introduction and Definition
What is Generative AI?
Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI or GenAI) refers to a specialized subset of artificial intelligence and machine learning that focuses on creating new content rather than simply analyzing or classifying existing data. While traditional AI systems are designed to recognize patterns, flag emails as spam, or categorize images, Generative AI takes things a step further by producing entirely original outputs—such as text, images, audio, video, and computer code—that mimic human creativity.

2. Working Principle & FlowchartAt its core, Generative AI works by learning the underlying patterns, structures, and statistical relationships of massive amounts of training data. When a user gives it an instruction, the model uses these learned patterns to predict and synthesize completely new, contextually relevant content.
